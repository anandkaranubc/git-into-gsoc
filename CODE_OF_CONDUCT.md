# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community. Many people here are making their first open-source contribution. Treat questions as welcome and mistakes as normal.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Encouraging spam contributions or fabricated GSoC applications
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer using the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/anandkaranubc). Please do not open a public issue for a conduct report. If the report concerns the maintainer, you can also use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

All reports are reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate. Repeated or serious violations can lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
